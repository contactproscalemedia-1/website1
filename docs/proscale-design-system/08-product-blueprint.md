# Product Blueprint

## Homepage Experience

The homepage is structured as a sequence of eight rooms. Each room should deliver a distinct layer of the argument: opening statement, verdict, engine, evidence, jurisdiction, operators, diagnosis, and decision.

## Room Responsibilities

- Opening Statement: establish the premise and tone
- Verdict: frame the business case and the seriousness of the subject
- Engine: explain the operating system and capabilities
- Evidence: present proof and credibility without fabricating results
- Jurisdiction: define the scope of authority and decision-making
- Operators: explain the team or operating model in a restrained way
- Diagnosis: identify the business issue or opportunity
- Decision: present the next action clearly and confidently

## Delivery Notes

Build one room at a time. Keep the experience coherent across transitions, and ensure each room has a clear purpose before adding visual complexity.
