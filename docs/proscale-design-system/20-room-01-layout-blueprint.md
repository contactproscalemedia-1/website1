## Layout

### Desktop

- 40% editorial
- 60% architectural environment
- Never equal columns
- The visual weight belongs to the typography

## Grid

### Maximum Width

- 1440px

### Content Width

- 1280px

### Horizontal Padding

- Desktop: 80px
- Tablet: 48px
- Mobile: 24px

---

## Vertical Rhythm

- Top spacing: 120px
- Between Eyebrow and H1: 24px
- Between H1 and Copy: 32px
- Between Copy and CTA: 40px
- Between CTA and Trust Indicator: 48px
- Bottom spacing: 120px

---

## Typography

### Eyebrow

- Inter
- Uppercase
- Increased letter spacing
- Small
- Muted

### H1

- Bebas Neue
- Very large
- Maximum four lines
- Never exceed 12 words
- Dominates the room

### Supporting Copy

- Inter
- 18–20px
- Maximum three lines
- Quiet
- Never promotional

### CTA

- Single primary button
- No secondary CTA

### Trust Indicator

- Small editorial line
- Examples:
  - Trusted by 50+ brands
  - Dubai • UAE
  - Performance Partner for Growth
- Keep understated

---

## Image Placement

- Occupies approximately 60% of the layout
- Always anchored right
- Never centered
- Never full-screen
- Must leave breathing room for typography

---

## Image Crop

### Desktop

- Wide editorial crop
- Table visible
- Architecture visible
- Negative space preserved

### Tablet

- Crop tighter
- Keep architecture

### Mobile

- Dedicated mobile composition
- Do not auto-crop desktop

---

## Navigation

- Transparent over hero
- Becomes solid only after scrolling
- Logo left
- Navigation right
- Single primary CTA

---

## Motion

- Fade
- Subtle translate
- Duration: 500–700ms
- No dramatic animations
- Reduced-motion preference fully supported

---

## Responsive Behaviour

- Desktop: split layout
- Tablet: 55 / 45 split
- Mobile: single column
- Typography first
- Image second
- CTA remains above the fold

---

## Accessibility

- Single H1
- Minimum AA contrast
- Visible keyboard focus
- Reduced motion respected
- Alt text required
- Logical reading order

---

## Success Criteria

The visitor immediately understands:

- This company makes strategic business decisions
- Typography dominates
- Architecture supports
- The room feels calm
- The room feels permanent
- Nothing competes for attention
- The first scroll creates curiosity rather than overload
