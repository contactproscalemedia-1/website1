# Opening Statement

## Status

Draft. Copy and visual direction require final approval before implementation.

## Mind Change

Before:
The visitor expects another marketing-agency website.

After:
The visitor understands that business performance, money, and accountability are already on the table.

## Emotional Objective

Calm with immediate authority.

## Copy Candidates

### Candidate A

PRESENCE
ISN'T PERFORMANCE.

Supporting copy:
Attention is visible.
Business impact is measurable.

CTA:
Examine the evidence

### Candidate B

EVERY DOLLAR
SHOULD ANSWER FOR ITSELF.

Supporting copy:
We measure what affects the business.

CTA:
Examine the evidence

## Opening Visual

One controlled architectural light treatment.
Prefer CSS light and shadow first.
Use a still image second.
Use video only if it creates meaningful additional value.

## Opening Motion

- Begin from black
- Logo or mark may fade in
- Headline receives the room's only character stagger
- Supporting copy and CTA enter after the headline
- Motion must end in stillness
- First visitor scroll cancels unfinished opening animation
- Reduced motion removes translate and character stagger
