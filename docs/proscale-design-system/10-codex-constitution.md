# Codex Constitution

## Governing Rules

1. Authority comes before attention.
2. The site should feel calm, precise, and commercially serious.
3. The implementation should support truth, clarity, and accountability.
4. Motion must serve understanding and should never become spectacle.
5. Accessibility and performance are not optional.
6. Build one room at a time and preserve unrelated working sections.
7. Do not invent claims, metrics, clients, testimonials, dates, budgets, or business outcomes.

## Design Review Standard

Before shipping a section, verify that it increases authority rather than merely increasing visual intensity. If a choice feels flashy, generic, or promotional, revise it.
