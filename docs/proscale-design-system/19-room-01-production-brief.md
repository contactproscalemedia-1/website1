# 19 — Room 01 Production Brief

## Room

01 – Opening Statement

## Environment

Executive Boardroom

## Status

Approved for Production

---

## Purpose

Room 01 creates the visitor's first impression.

It must immediately communicate that ProScaleMEDIA is a company built on strategic decisions, measurable performance, and disciplined execution.

The visitor should feel they have entered the executive headquarters of a performance company.

---

## Emotional Objective

The visitor should feel:

- Calm
- Confident
- Curious
- Safe
- Impressed by discipline rather than decoration

They should never feel overwhelmed or sold to.

---

## Key Message

Performance begins with decisions.

Everything inside the room should support this idea.

---

## Environment

Modern executive boardroom.

- Architectural concrete
- Dark oak
- Smoked glass
- Black steel
- Natural stone
- Museum-quality interior

The space should not feel like:

- A luxury hotel
- A startup office
- A coworking space

---

## Composition

- Landscape
- 16:9
- Editorial composition
- Left side reserved for typography
- Right side contains the architectural focal point
- Large negative space
- The room itself is the hero

---

## Camera

- Eye level
- 35–50mm equivalent
- Medium-format editorial perspective
- No distortion
- Natural proportions

---

## Lighting

- Morning sunlight
- Single dominant light source
- Long architectural shadows
- Soft reflections
- Natural exposure
- No HDR
- No bloom
- No artificial cinematic lighting

---

## Materials

- Concrete
- Dark oak
- Smoked glass
- Black steel
- Leather
- Stone

Everything should feel permanent.

---

## Required Objects

- Long executive conference table
- Leather notebook
- Fountain pen
- Glass of water
- Minimal integrated performance display
- One chair slightly pulled back

Nothing else.

---

## Performance Display

The display should be built into the architecture.

It should not feel:

- Mounted like a television
- Floating
- Futuristic

It should display only essential metrics. Examples:

- Ad Spend
- ROAS
- Revenue
- Qualified Leads

Use restrained red accents inspired by the logo.

---

## Logo Integration

The logo should never dominate the room.

If visible, it should appear as subtle environmental branding. Examples:

- Wall engraving
- Reception plaque
- Architectural signage
- Frosted glass

It should never appear as:

- A large floating logo
- Decorative wallpaper

The upward arrow should inspire the architectural language rather than become a graphic element repeated throughout the room.

---

## Color Palette

95% neutral.

- Concrete grey
- Warm charcoal
- Black
- Oak brown
- Off-white

Maximum 5% ProScaleMEDIA red.

Red exists only to guide attention.

---

## Visual Hierarchy

1. Architecture
2. Conference table
3. Natural light
4. Performance display
5. Human traces (notebook, pen, glass)

The visitor should notice the room before noticing any object.

---

## Forbidden Elements

- No people
- No smiling business teams
- No handshakes
- No laptops as hero objects
- No coffee cups
- No visible cables
- No RGB lighting
- No neon
- No holograms
- No exaggerated luxury
- No generic office furniture
- No fake dashboards
- No marketing clichés

---

## Higgsfield Prompt

Create an ultra-realistic editorial photograph of the executive boardroom inside ProScaleMEDIA headquarters.

The room features architectural concrete walls, dark oak paneling, smoked glass, black steel details, natural stone flooring, and a long executive conference table as the central element.

Warm morning sunlight enters through full-height glass walls, casting soft tree shadows across the architecture.

A leather notebook, fountain pen, and a single glass of water create subtle human presence.

An architectural performance display is seamlessly integrated into the wall, showing only four restrained metrics: Ad Spend, ROAS, Revenue, and Qualified Leads, using minimal ProScaleMEDIA red accents.

The composition reserves generous negative space on the left for editorial typography while the architecture remains the hero of the scene.

The atmosphere feels calm, disciplined, permanent, and executive.

Editorial architectural photography.

Hasselblad medium-format aesthetic.

Natural materials.

Museum-quality realism.

No AI artifacts.

---

## Negative Prompt

- People
- Teams
- Handshakes
- Coffee mugs
- Open laptops
- Messy desks
- RGB lighting
- Neon
- Cyberpunk
- Startup offices
- Coworking spaces
- Luxury hotel lobbies
- Floating interfaces
- Sci-fi dashboards
- Stock-photo composition
- Excessive decoration
- Artificial HDR
- Over-sharpening

---

## Success Criteria

- The room feels photographed rather than generated.
- The architecture communicates authority.
- The room feels like the headquarters of a performance company.
- Typography can be added without fighting the composition.
- The image remains timeless.
- Removing the logo would not reduce the perceived quality or identity of the space.
- The visitor should immediately understand that important business decisions are made here.
