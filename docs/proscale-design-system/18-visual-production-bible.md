# 18 — Visual Production Bible

## Purpose

This document defines the visual DNA of ProScaleMEDIA.

It is the source of truth for every visual asset created for the brand, regardless of the production method.

It applies equally to:

- Higgsfield
- Photography
- Film
- 3D Rendering
- AI Image Generation
- Motion Design
- Website Imagery
- Social Media
- Advertising
- Presentation Decks

The goal is consistency.

Every visual should feel like it belongs to the same company.

## Brand Emotion

Every image should communicate:

- Calm
- Authority
- Discipline
- Precision
- Confidence
- Strategic thinking
- Measured execution

Never excitement.
Never hype.
Never chaos.

## The World of ProScaleMEDIA

The visitor is not browsing a marketing agency.

The visitor is entering the headquarters of a performance company.

Every room should feel connected.
The website is an architectural journey.
Each section represents another department inside the same building.

## Headquarters Concept

The headquarters consists of spaces such as:

- Executive Boardroom
- Evidence Gallery
- Strategy Library
- Operations Center
- Industry Exhibition
- Decision Room

Every environment belongs inside one coherent building.
Never generate unrelated locations.

## Visual Philosophy

The image supports the message.

Typography leads.
Photography reinforces.
Decoration is removed.
Everything visible must have a purpose.

## Photography Style

- Editorial
- Architectural
- Luxury
- Timeless
- Natural

Never theatrical.
Never cinematic for the sake of drama.

Think:

- Apple
- Leica
- Porsche
- Aman
- Wallpaper*
- Kinfolk
- Financial Times
- McKinsey

Not:

- Agency websites
- Startup landing pages
- Crypto
- Futuristic interfaces

## Camera Language

### Preferred Cameras

- Medium Format
- Large Sensor Editorial Look

### Preferred Feel

- Hasselblad X2D
- Phase One
- Leica SL
- Arri Alexa (video)

## Lens Language

### Preferred

- 35mm
- 40mm
- 50mm

### Avoid

- Ultra-wide distortion
- Fish-eye
- Extreme telephoto compression

The perspective should always feel natural.

## Camera Position

- Eye level
- Human perspective
- No drone shots
- No impossible camera angles
- No exaggerated perspective

## Composition

- Editorial
- Structured
- Balanced
- Quiet
- Use strong geometry
- Leading lines
- Architectural symmetry
- Large negative space

The subject must breathe.

## Lighting

Natural light whenever possible.

### Preferred

- Morning
- Soft daylight
- Single dominant light source
- Controlled shadows
- Subtle reflections

### Avoid

- Bloom
- Lens flare
- Artificial glow
- Colored lighting
- RGB lighting
- Heavy volumetrics
- Over-dramatic contrast

## Color Palette

### Primary

- Deep charcoal
- Soft black
- Warm white
- Muted grey

### Accent

- ProScale Red

Accent usage should remain minimal.
Red guides attention.
It never dominates.

## Materials

### Preferred

- Architectural concrete
- Dark oak
- Travertine
- Black steel
- Smoked glass
- Leather
- Natural stone
- Brushed aluminum

### Avoid

- Chrome-heavy interiors
- Plastic furniture
- Cheap office materials
- Futuristic surfaces
- Neon materials

## Objects

### Allowed

- Leather notebook
- Executive documents
- Architectural books
- Glass of water
- Fountain pen
- Minimal stationery
- Single chair slightly moved
- Performance display

### Avoid

- Coffee mugs
- Messy desks
- Multiple laptops
- Visible cables
- People shaking hands
- Marketing props
- Plants used as decoration
- Bright accessories
- Anything that feels staged

## Human Presence

People are optional.

If present:

- Never looking at camera
- Never smiling for marketing
- Never posing

The environment should suggest human intelligence without depending on people.

Often an empty room is stronger.

## Performance Displays

Displays should feel believable.

- Minimal
- Quiet
- Real

### Examples

- Ad Spend
- ROAS
- Qualified Leads
- Revenue
- Conversion Rate

### Avoid

- Fake dashboards
- Random charts
- Sci-fi interfaces
- Neon graphics
- Decorative analytics

## Typography Integration

Typography always owns the composition.

Photography never competes with typography.

Images must provide calm areas for text placement.

## Motion

Movement should feel physical.

- Camera moves slowly
- Objects remain still
- Never create movement simply because animation is possible

Motion should resemble documentary filmmaking.

## Texture

Imperfection creates authenticity.

### Allow

- Natural reflections
- Tiny surface variation
- Soft material wear
- Tree shadows
- Glass reflections
- Small asymmetry

### Avoid

- Perfect CGI surfaces
- Sterile symmetry
- Artificial perfection

## Image Quality

- Editorial quality
- Museum quality
- Magazine quality
- Large dynamic range
- Sharp materials
- Soft natural lighting
- No AI artifacts
- No over-sharpening
- No excessive HDR

## Color Grading

- Warm neutral
- Deep blacks
- Natural whites
- Muted saturation
- No teal/orange grading
- No Instagram look
- No trendy LUTs

## Negative Space

Luxury equals restraint.

If additional visual elements are unnecessary, remove them.

Empty space communicates confidence.

## Storytelling

Every image answers one question:

"What important decision is made here?"

Never:

"What looks impressive?"

## Production Workflow

1. Creative Direction
2. Mood Board
3. Visual Production
4. Review
5. Approval
6. Website Integration
7. Engineering

Never reverse this order.

## Quality Checklist

Before approving any image ask:

- Does it feel photographed rather than generated?
- Would Apple approve this composition?
- Would Leica publish this image?
- Does it communicate authority?
- Does it avoid marketing clichés?
- Is every object intentional?
- Could this image exist ten years from now?

If any answer is "No", revise before production.

## Final Principle

We do not create beautiful images.

We create environments that make important business decisions feel inevitable.

Every visual asset should make the visitor feel they have been invited into a room where important business decisions are made—not into a room trying to impress them.

---

**Visual Motto**

Build trust before admiration.

Confidence before spectacle.

Substance before decoration.
