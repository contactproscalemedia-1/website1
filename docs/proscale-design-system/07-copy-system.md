# Copy System

## Voice and Tone

The tone should be calm, direct, and accountable. Copy should sound like the business is already serious about performance and money, not like a marketing agency trying to be clever.

## Writing Rules

- Prefer clarity over flourish
- Use simple statements with strong structure
- Keep claims factual and non-fabricated
- Let numbers carry weight when they are relevant
- Avoid hype, exaggeration, and generic superlatives

## Approved Direction

Use language that reinforces the core idea that business impact is measurable and that attention alone is not performance.

## Copy Patterns

- Short, declarative headlines
- Supporting sentences that clarify rather than decorate
- Calls to action that invite examination, review, or proof
- Minimal use of adjectives and modifiers

## Implementation Notes

Copy should be designed to work in tandem with the visual system. It should feel precise, grounded, and confident without sounding stiff or overly corporate.
