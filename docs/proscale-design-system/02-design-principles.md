# Design Principles

## Authority Over Attention

The highest priority is authority. A page that looks exciting but feels less credible should be revised. Visual intensity must support confidence, not replace it.

## Editorial Restraint

The interface should feel deliberate and controlled. Remove unnecessary decoration. Use composition, spacing, and hierarchy to create emphasis.

## Black, White, and Red Only

The palette is limited to black, white, and red. Red should be used selectively for emphasis and direction, not as a dominant surface treatment.

## Structural Honesty

The design should feel architectural. It should look built and intentional, with clear relationships between parts of the page.

## Typography as Architecture

Typography carries meaning. Headlines should feel monumental and authoritative, while body copy should remain readable and calm.

## Motion Must Earn Its Place

Motion should support arrival, departure, and feedback. It should never distract from the content or create theatrical motion for its own sake.

## Content Must Be Real

Do not fabricate results, clients, testimonials, dates, budgets, or outcomes. The design system should support truthfulness and measurement.
